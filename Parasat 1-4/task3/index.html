Nikola Tesla

About Nikola Tesla

1856 – 1943

Nikola Tesla was a brilliant inventor, electrical engineer, and futurist. He was born in 1856 in the Austrian Empire and passed away in 1943 in New York. His groundbreaking work and inventions revolutionized the way the world uses electrical energy today.

Important Life Events

  1. Born in Smiljan in 1856.
  2. Emigrated to the United States in 1884.
  3. Developed the AC electrical system in the late 1880s.
  4. Invented the Tesla coil in 1891.
  5. Passed away in New York City in 1943.

Key Contributions

  • Alternating Current (AC) electricity
  • The Tesla Coil
  • Induction motor concepts
  • Early radio technology contributions
